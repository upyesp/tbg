To Boldly Go helps blind and low-vision travellers follow the journeys they plan — step by step, stop by stop, in plain language, offline.

Open the app

Who it’s for

Anyone who travels with a screen reader, low vision, or a busy mind. Every screen is uncluttered, high-contrast, and announced in the order you need it.

What it does

  • Plan journeys from your saved places and a catalogue of everyday places — stations, toilets, pharmacies, and more.
  • Follow a journey with spoken guidance: what’s next, how far, and on which clock-face direction.
  • Work offline — your journeys live on your device after you sign in.
  • Stay private — sign in with a passkey. No email, no username, no password, ever.

Accessibility commitment

Built for screen readers first: NVDA, VoiceOver, and TalkBack are first-class users. High contrast (7:1) in light and dark themes, large targets, and calm, predictable layouts. Automated accessibility checks run on every change.
